# Yearly Log Prompt

Create the Life Log retrospective for the most recently completed calendar year in `America/Chicago`.

Read `AGENTS.md` first and follow it as authoritative guidance.

## Source material

Use the completed monthly summaries as the primary source. Read weekly or daily files only when necessary to recover important detail or resolve chronology.

Create:

`yearly/YYYY.md`

## Synthesis goals

Tell the story of the year as a trajectory, not twelve separate month summaries.

Capture:

- the year's major chapters
- turning points
- career movement
- products, projects, and systems built or abandoned
- skills and ideas that compounded
- important decisions and changes in direction
- major wins, failures, and periods of friction
- recurring themes that were invisible day to day
- memorable and funny moments worth retaining
- what materially changed between the beginning and end of the year
- the most important unresolved threads entering the next year

Use quarters or thematic chapters when they improve the narrative.

## Historical integrity

Do not rewrite lower-level files. The yearly retrospective is the strongest hindsight layer, but it should remain grounded in the canonical chronology below it.

## Style

Write a selective, vivid retrospective worth rereading years later. Compress routine detail heavily. Preserve moments that explain how the year actually felt and why it mattered.

## Final response

After the repository write succeeds, respond only with a concise confirmation containing the year and repository path created or updated.
