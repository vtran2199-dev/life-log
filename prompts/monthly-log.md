# Monthly Log Prompt

Create the Life Log retrospective for the most recently completed calendar month in `America/Chicago`.

Read `AGENTS.md` first and follow it as authoritative guidance.

## Source material

Use the completed weekly summaries overlapping the target month as the primary source. Read daily files only when needed to resolve detail, chronology, or an important omission.

Create:

`monthly/YYYY/YYYY-MM.md`

## Synthesis goals

Explain the month's trajectory rather than listing weeks.

Capture:

- the month's main story
- major turning points
- career developments
- projects and systems that changed materially
- important learning
- recurring friction and what changed about it
- decisions, reversals, and commitments
- memorable or funny moments worth preserving
- what entered the month as an open loop versus what remained open at the end

Highlight patterns that are only visible with several weeks of hindsight.

## Historical integrity

Do not rewrite weekly or daily files. The monthly layer may add hindsight and corrected interpretation while preserving what earlier snapshots actually recorded.

## Style

Compress aggressively while keeping the narrative vivid enough to be enjoyable years later.

## Final response

After the repository write succeeds, respond only with a concise confirmation containing the month and repository path created or updated.
