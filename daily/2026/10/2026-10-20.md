### JobCRM Outbound: from mockup to production, then a reality check

Victor directed an **AE-first, existing-CRM-opportunities-first** prospecting workflow: **Discovery → Copy GPT Review Packet → Import GPT Review → Outbound Queue**. GPT would judge people as **Priority, Fallback, or Not Relevant**, while code would handle exact IDs, validation, deduplication, and persistence. The Queue should feel as fast as Apply Queue, log LinkedIn requests and messages manually, and never change an application's stage just because outreach occurred. The planning and mockup work had been pushed in commits including **`760d37b`, `336cdb9`, `3d5e444`, and `7ed77e1`**.

Implementation then advanced beyond a mockup. A reported build checkpoint **`11d7e3a`** passed isolated Neon checks; the production rollout included **migration 0063**, **48 focused tests**, and a local Playwright pass. Initial production data exposed a flaw: ten saved posts yielded **zero eligible author profiles** under overly restrictive filtering. An eligibility correction was then reported deployed in **`976e036`, `914948f`, and `a7f5ae3`**, with **13 tests / 48 assertions**, targeted lint/build, and a production packet/reload check passing. The corrected review surfaced **nine Dialpad candidates**: **zero Priority, two Fallback, seven Not Relevant**. The two fallback people, **Ken Martin and Lindy Cabajog**, appeared in the live Outbound Queue, establishing that the review-to-queue path was usable.

That success immediately exposed the next defect. Both prospects' LinkedIn locations were in the **Philippines**, not the target US hiring geography, and their associated Dialpad application was already **Rejected**. The Queue did not adequately surface application/rejection context and lacked a working **Dismiss/Restore** control. Victor wanted to remove irrelevant prospects from his active queue **without deleting evidence or losing the ability to restore them**. Those were identified fixes, **not verified as shipped on October 20**. The result was a genuine working slice of the product, not a trustworthy fully qualified hiring-manager pipeline.

### Enterprise BDR audit: 115 THIN did not mean 115 good jobs

Victor investigated whether the large JobCRM fallback pool concealed better-paid **Enterprise, Strategic, Senior, or Commercial/Mid-Market BDR/SDR** openings. A read-only audit started from about **6,500 fallback records**, including **612 BDR/SDR title matches** plus **57 Ashby matches**. A more selective inventory found **152 stored role matches: 37 FULL and 115 THIN**. Of the THIN records, **72** had plausible fallback reasons, **42** were within the global **90-day** freshness window, and **18** were linked to Opportunities. These figures are filters and overlaps, **not numbers to add together**; the 42 were an **investigation/hydration pool**, not 42 confirmed good live jobs.

A small four-board listing test made **20 requests** and found **23 matching live postings**. **Fifteen** were both recent and clearly Texas/Remote-US eligible: **13 newly discovered ordinary SDR listings**, one existing **Sardine Enterprise BDR THIN** with a **$75,000–$95,000 base** range, and one existing **Influ2 Enterprise SDR FULL/Opportunity**. The test found **zero new Enterprise/Strategic/Senior postings**. No hydration, promotion, provider-wide scan, database write, or CRM seeding was performed. Victor wanted to know whether he could use the existing normal review flow instead of building another pipeline for a tiny qualified set.

## Decisions / Things Learned

- **Completed interviews outrank casual distractions**, and future steps must not be logged as offers or completed interviews.
- **Sales hiring is itself a prospecting market:** find timely hiring evidence, connect with the actual decision-maker, and preserve context rather than spraying applications.
- **A functioning queue is not a qualified queue.** Rejection stage, location, person relevance, and recoverable dismissal must remain visible.
- **42 fresh THIN records are candidates for verification**, not automatically 42 jobs worth hydrating or applying to.
- A simple **lock-screen recording** is more useful for dream recall than a sophisticated system that never captures a dream.
