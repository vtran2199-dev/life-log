### A friend's used-camera operation revealed a different automation opportunity

A long evening conversation explored a friend's **GADJIT used-camera/electronics resale business**. Victor saw the connection to his job-board scanning experience: AI could triage a large listing feed and present **high-confidence, demand-matched opportunities** for a human to approve. ## Things Learned / Decisions

- **Submission is the finish line for an assignment.** A polished PDF, a draft email, and a mock-call plan are different states from an Ashby submission.
- **A completed interview is not a next-round offer**, and an invitation is not an attended meeting. Record those separately.
- **Production deployment, MCP review import, and paid scraping are three different milestones**; prove each one independently.
- **The bottleneck in resale sourcing is selecting profitable, demanded inventory**, not maximizing how many listings an AI can scrape.
- **AI research is most useful when its hypotheses are visibly separate from buyer evidence**, as with Ford's HR postings and LinkedIn signals.
