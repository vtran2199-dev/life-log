### Sales, leverage, and the type of work Victor actually wants

Victor kept unpacking why some outbound work energizes him while generic BDR work feels like an enormous step backward. His own restaurant prospecting had worked best when he could demonstrate a striking, concrete product improvement on the spot, not merely interrupt people with a script. He revisited lessons from HighRadius, university career-fair prospecting, and AteOrder: he likes building an information advantage, seeing the opportunity, and personally capturing the upside.

The discussion clarified a recurring preference for **GTM systems plus actual revenue ownership**. He wants to leverage AI, automation, territory intelligence and strong discovery in service of closing meaningful deals, rather than become a back-office operator with no commercial upside or a BDR forever. He also challenged whether real estate, car sales, and software sales reward different kinds of prospecting, differentiation and repeatable value.
