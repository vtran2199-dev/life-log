Victor spent a memorable stretch probing GPT-6's new behavior, interface possibilities, memory, cross-chat context retrieval, and whether the assistant really knew the backstory of a reference to Isaac and “100 calls.” He deliberately stress-tested apparent understanding instead of assuming that confident recognition meant accurate retrieval.

Victor was struck by the potential to move from screenshots to functioning HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototypes and eventually integrate those designs into JobCRM, while keeping a distinction between a demo with hardcoded data and a real application connected to its backend.

## Things Learned

- For Sparta, defend the research method, prioritization, and opening questions without manufacturing prospect pain, and be ready to connect that work to the unglamorous daily call volume.
- AI-assisted prospecting can improve targeting, but it does not replace calls or the job of generating meetings.
- An RVP discussion is only a possible next step until it is actually confirmed.
- Treat active interview progress separately from offers and onboarding obligations.
- Keep raw activity in a durable system and use summaries as derived views, not competing sources of truth.
- Screenshot-to-prototype can accelerate design, but actual state, data, and validation still need engineering.
- Ask for evidence when a viral image or AI-recalled detail seems too surprising to trust.
