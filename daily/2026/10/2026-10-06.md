The dogfood also exposed a deeper identity problem. Ordinary Lever board tokens were being treated too cautiously, sending normal companies into manual naming work. Victor settled on a narrower rule:

- ordinary New boards should default to the raw upstream token
- strip only the provider prefixes such as `global:` or `eu:`
- prefer the exact `upstreamToken`
- do not invent normalization heuristics
- keep suspicious aggregators such as Jobgether unresolved
- do not change Registered company identities
- repair existing unresolved records in place instead of rehydrating and creating duplicates

## Things Learned

- A manual confirmation gate can become counterproductive when it treats ordinary provider tokens as suspicious.
- The safest Lever identity rule is narrow and source-faithful: exact upstream token, strip only known provider prefixes, and isolate true exceptions.
- Email classification is already useful, but durable value requires incremental state and CRM reconciliation.
- A one-character SQL mistake can block an otherwise carefully designed import pipeline.
- Good interview preparation is not more research forever. It is knowing the thesis, defending the ranking, and rehearsing how to earn the meeting.
- Raw prospecting signals, canonical ranking, presentation output, and practice material are different artifacts and should stay separate.
- A résumé file can be finished while its delivery and tracking infrastructure is still unfinished.
- The next layer of leverage is not another tool; it is a disciplined review of whether the current tools are helping.
