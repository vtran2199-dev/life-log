## Things Learned / Decisions

- **Finish the real sales loop:** evidence of hiring → identifiable person → connection requested → accepted → messaged → responded. Store each step and its time without pretending a request is a conversation.
- **A completed batch is not complete market coverage.** Keep searched, unsearched, reviewed, imported, and contacted counts separate.
- **Reuse extensible architecture before changing code.** The fifth builder worked with a fresh import, not a refactor.
- **Prototype cheaply, then move to the tools already available.** GADJIT did not need Neon or a real eBay integration to show its concept.
- **Small automations can deliver disproportionate joy** when they work immediately instead of becoming an all-night debugging session.
