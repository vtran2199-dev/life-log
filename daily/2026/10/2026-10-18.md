### Job search: find the hiring post, then the human

After reaching a self-reported roughly **1,050–1,100 job applications** over the wider search, Victor was tired of waiting for applicant-tracking systems to decide his future. He wanted to use JobCRM like an outbound sales platform to reach the people hiring **SMB and mid-market Account Executives**. His signed **Multiview** offer, with a November 10 start in Irving, remained a fallback with relocation and job-fit problems.

The strategy became unusually precise: **find a recent LinkedIn post from someone actively hiring AEs, identify the person behind the post, and contact them directly**. Only after exhausting strong post-based leads should he fall back to sales managers matched to specific open jobs, with recruiters as a further backup. Victor pushed back hard when a suggested design reversed that sequence and started collecting managers without hiring-post evidence. He wanted the **seven-day freshest posts prioritized**, not an endless list of people who might not be hiring.

He explored **Apify, Clay, LinkedIn, and JobCRM** as parts of one practical workflow: discover and rank real hiring signals, preserve company/post/author evidence and costs, enrich only useful contacts, then send human-approved outreach. The current UI's five-company batches, company LinkedIn identity requirements, and potential repeated paid searches were clear weaknesses. A 90-day search range could provide context, but should not bury fresh posts.

## Decisions / Things Learned

- **Recent hiring posts are stronger signals than generic manager lists.** Follow the post to its author before looking for colder contacts.
- **A live search result is not a verified job opening**, and a deployed component is not an end-to-end working outreach system.
- Keep **human approval** on outreach, remember searches already paid for, and test a small batch before scaling.
- Design for the **phone actually used**: compact Planner controls, one-tap imports, and lock-screen recording.
- Classify actual secret exposure before stopping work over a **credential fingerprint**.
