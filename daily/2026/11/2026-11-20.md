## What Happened

The meeting did establish some important facts. **Gusto showed a $75,000 annual salary effective November 16**, rather than the $70,000 base Victor believed he had negotiated. Becky confirmed the payroll and linked bank details, the **November 30 payroll expectation**, and a phone allowance. Training assignments and benefits questions were not all settled.

Victor wanted to preserve his preexisting coding, job-CRM and trading projects rather than inadvertently assigning them to Sparta. ### The prospecting system: JobCRM meets Sparta's HubSpot

Victor spent a substantial part of the day asking how to get good at the Americas SDR job—not merely how to make forty calls a day. One of the AEs had emphasized a valuable signal: **when someone who used Sparta at one trading company moves to another, follow that champion**. Victor was astonished that there was not an obvious systematic way to watch for these moves. He explored LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Clay, Apify, public LinkedIn posts, job-change alerts and HubSpot contact histories.

His existing **JobCRM** project already contains job-board integrations for **Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever and SmartRecruiters**, plus LinkedIn/public-post discovery capabilities. He investigated whether parts of that research infrastructure could help detect trader hiring, desk expansion, leadership changes and former customers moving companies. The immediate answer was to understand the tools Sparta already pays for and the ownership rules before building a duplicate pipeline.

A HubSpot investigation surfaced **3,154 companies**, **406 tagged Americas**, and **222 Americas-tagged companies without an owner**, while Victor himself had **zero assigned company records**. An estimated Americas universe of roughly **950 accounts** was discussed, but those figures were exploratory and tags were incomplete; **unowned did not mean available to claim or call**. Florencio's accounts, open opportunities, active sequences and existing relationships would all need checking before prospecting. Victor was still in training, with live calling expected later, not launching a completed outbound campaign Friday.

He also audited his **company-managed Claude** environment: The Spine, HubSpot, Slack, Confluence and Granola were available in some form; ZoomInfo and Clay were not connected there, and Outreach needed reconnecting. He wanted sensible **global instructions for research, learning and general business use first**, with prospecting support later—not a bloated, prospecting-only AI persona. He explicitly held off connecting his personal GitHub repository to company Claude until access, retention, intellectual-property and privacy boundaries were clearer. **No new scraper, CRM automation or GitHub deployment was confirmed.**
