## What Happened

### Sparta: from onboarding information to a workable sales plan

Tuesday was Victor's second calendar day after starting as Sparta Commodities' Americas SDR on Monday. The central question shifted from whether he could start to **how quickly he could become productive**. He wanted to finish **Sparta Ascend**, learn the minimum product and market knowledge needed to call intelligently, listen to useful HubSpot examples, practice with roleplays, and secure an approved prospect list and calling access. This was a plan for the ramp, **not evidence that he had completed certification or begun a full outbound campaign**.

He explored the software and sales operations behind that goal: **HubSpot, Outreach, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Clay, Leonidas**, and internal knowledge systems such as **Ascend, Confluence and Atlassian**. He noted that Sparta had used Outreach relatively little and wanted to move away from a tool costing roughly **$75,000 annually**, because HubSpot now appeared capable of handling some of the same work. This was an understanding to discuss with Isaac, **not a completed migration or confirmed savings**. Victor also wanted access to Leonidas and a chance to use **simulated/paper trading** to better understand how commodity traders think and operate. No trading account setup or simulated trade was confirmed Tuesday.

The account-territory question mattered more than the tool list. Victor reviewed calls from former BDR **Florencio** and worried about how much net-new opportunity would actually remain if account expansion and previously worked records were reassigned in 2027. He wanted to distinguish available net-new accounts, existing CRM opportunities, ownership rules and realistic pipeline capacity before committing to an activity plan.

### What comes after SDR?

Victor revisited conversations and interview recordings with Isaac to clarify the role between SDR and full AE. The title he was trying to remember was **BDM (Business Development Manager)**, also described as **BDE**: a proposed intermediate role that could own and close smaller deals rather than only set meetings. The discussion suggested a potential **2027** bridge toward AE, with a typical **12–18-month** progression and a possible faster route around six months for strong performers. Those figures were discussed as possibilities, **not a promotion promise, fixed timetable or job offer**. The larger career goal remained clear: become a closer, not remain indefinitely in a meeting-setting role.

### Why can Finder drag but Claude and Teams cannot?

The day's most infuriating small problem was macOS window management. Victor could drag **Finder** windows normally with his **external mouse**, but **Claude Desktop** and **Microsoft Teams** sometimes refused to move even after repeated attempts. He shared a screenshot and inspected the app-specific title-bar behavior on his **16-inch M5 Pro MacBook Pro with 48 GB RAM**. The discussion considered whether custom title bars or a macOS regression were involved, but **no confirmed fix or root cause** emerged. It was the sort of tiny interface failure that feels absurdly expensive when it happens on a brand-new high-end laptop.
