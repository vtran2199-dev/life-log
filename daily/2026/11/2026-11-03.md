## What Happened

### Boom: straight to the CEO

Victor interviewed with **Boom CEO Rob Whiting** around **9:30–9:52 a.m. Central**, after researching Rob's background and Boom's business. He drew on his NetSuite record (including being **#7 among 700-plus**), high-volume prospecting, territory systems, competitive tennis, and endurance training. ### Sparta: interrogating the actual job, not just the offer letter

The written Sparta SDR offer remained **$65K base, approximately $40K target variable / $105K OTE, 50 options, November 16 start**. Victor pressed for **$80K or more base**, at times imagining **$90K**, and wanted to avoid another low-leverage prospecting treadmill. He examined Americas territory size, account ownership, quota of approximately **10 meetings per quarter**, inbound versus outbound credit, and whether HubSpot, Clay, ZoomInfo, old recordings, and dormant accounts could make the role more productive.

The deeper research surfaced uncomfortable contrasts: earlier SDRs reportedly made around **30 calls a week**, versus a proposed **850 calls a month**; about **150 neglected European opportunities** had been reassigned and some reactivated. He questioned prior SDR compensation, the usefulness of the account pool, whether 2027 commission changes were approved, and how credible the **12–18 month** advancement discussion was. A BDM path reportedly mixed roughly **80% SDR activity** with a few small full-cycle accounts, rather than a clean AE promotion. He also examined vesting, benefits, insurance, options, and whether a future GTM-engineering role was substantive. Isaac found a time for a **Wednesday, November 4** discussion; that call **had not happened Tuesday**. A requested offer extension to **Monday, November 9 at 5 p.m. Central** was not yet a confirmed approval.

Victor kept the signed **Multiview November 10** start as a fallback while comparing it with Sparta's November 16 start. He repeatedly returned to the inflation comparison: **$65K today felt too much like his $50K NetSuite base in 2020**, not the promotion he wanted.
