This was particularly aggravating because the laptop's delivery had already been used to justify moving the planned start from November 16 to November 23. His interest was pragmatic: low-spread **E8 prop-firm forex pairs** such as EUR/USD and USD/JPY, along with indices, historical price/time series, APIs, database design, and testing whether patterns hold up rather than simply drawing attractive charts.

The repository catch-up placed **AstroView's latest commits in September**, with a responsive interface, real geocentric planetary positions and aspects, and Moon-phase calculations already built. **Heliocentric geometry, market data integration, a trading database and automated execution remained unbuilt**. Friday's accomplishment was renewed research and scoping, **not a new commit or deployment**. He expected to balance the idea with Sparta and the Gadjit opportunity.
