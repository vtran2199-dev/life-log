# November 2, 2026

## The Day in One Sentence

Monday delivered Sparta's long-awaited written offer, an emphatic Boom mock-call win, rapid Redis advancement, and a bizarre last-minute Agicap cancellation, leaving Victor juggling real options instead of merely hoping for interviews.

## What Happened

### Sparta: the offer finally became real, but the details still mattered

After the verbal SDR offer on Friday, Sparta Commodities finally delivered its written offer on Monday, November 2. Three BambooHR notifications arrived and Chris apologized for the delay. The offer specified a **$65,000 base**, **November 16 start**, and **Wednesday, November 4 at 5 p.m.** acceptance deadline. Previously discussed figures included roughly **$105,000 OTE** and **50 options**, but the documents Victor reviewed did not clearly establish the individualized variable plan and equity terms. The packet described **25 vacation days plus holidays**, a **5% 401(k) match**, health benefits, and four-year equity vesting. Those benefits did not resolve the missing commission plan, insurance costs, option strike price, or exercise terms.

Victor considered pushing the base toward **$80,000** while protecting approximately **$40,000 variable**, and wanted to talk to Isaac about account coverage, territory, advancement into AE work, and a possible GTM-engineering track. Chris offered to consider an extension and requested questions in writing. Victor sent the questions, requested a **Monday, November 9** decision extension, and sought a Wednesday conversation with Isaac. Neither the extension nor revised economics was confirmed by day's end. He ranked **Sparta first, Boom second, Multiview third, and Agicap fourth** at one point. The signed Multiview **November 10** start remained an important fallback, not something he had canceled.

### Boom: Jacob said he crushed it

Victor spent the morning rehearsing a Senior BDR cold call for **Knightvest Capital's Nancy Casteel**, using its three Round Rock acquisitions totaling **1,027 units** as an entry point into leasing and applicant-screening friction. Practice was messy: simulated prospects were evasive, a roleplay became confused, and he worked on sharper discovery, specific meeting closes, and phrases like *time to decision* and *leasing velocity*.

The real **11:15 a.m. Central** mock-call interview with **Jacob Walker** went far better. Jacob's feedback was that Victor **“crushed it”**; the call exercises demonstrated that he could take coaching and improve quickly. He advanced toward a **Tuesday, November 3** conversation with CEO **Rob Whiting**, with a possible onsite and an accelerated hiring path still ahead. This was a strong interview result, not a Boom offer.

### Redis: an interview and an unusually fast next round

Victor completed the **1 p.m.** Redis interview with **Sharon Serviere**. The discussion covered a potentially **Senior SDR** role, roughly **$61,500 base / $87,500 OTE**, uncapped variable, a team target of **12 qualified opportunities per quarter**, coaching, and a new **Associate Account Executive** apprenticeship-style route into running smaller deals and shadowing sellers. Victor liked the sales-cycle exposure but noted that the compensation trailed his strongest alternatives.

Redis followed up rapidly, advancing him to a **45-minute cross-functional interview** and requesting availability for November 3–4. Skipping a cold-call exercise was discussed, not guaranteed. There was no Redis offer on Monday.

### Agicap: a final interview that never actually happened

Victor prepared to explain his real discovery with Tyler at **GADJIT** to Agicap CRO **Mickaël Jordan** and Tracy. The qualification conclusion was deliberately candid: estimated **$3M–$5M revenue**, below Agicap's stated **$10M–$500M** target, and no demonstrated urgent treasury problem despite multiple banks, card-financed inventory, QuickBooks, and spreadsheets. A thoughtful disqualification was more defensible than inventing a sales opportunity.

Instead, Mickaël called unexpectedly: **Tracy had an emergency and could not attend** the scheduled **4–5 p.m.** final. The interview was postponed to **Wednesday, November 4 at 1 p.m. Central**, with Mickaël suggesting a go/no-go could follow soon after. Victor was furious at the late change after extensive preparation and the time spent with Tyler. He also questioned whether Agicap's SDR role involved too much discovery for compensation Mickaël described as more realistically **$80K–$90K OTE**. He increasingly felt he could gain discovery experience at Sparta or Boom without making Agicap his priority.

### Other career and project threads

Multiview's **Hope Davis** sent a warm pre-start note for November 10.
